> I am pleased to participate as a speaker in the **1st Summer School in Robotics & AI**, which will take place June 29–30, 2026 at the Eugenides Foundation in Athens.

The summer school is organized by **HERON**, the Hellenic Robotics Center of Excellence and ATHENA RC, in collaboration with the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology (KIT). The event will bring together researchers, engineers, and students to explore recent developments in robotics and artificial intelligence.

The program will include talks on legged robotics and agile mobility, and I look forward to engaging with participants and discussing current research challenges in the field.

Applications for the summer school are open until March 30, 2026.
