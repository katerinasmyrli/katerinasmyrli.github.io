> I will be involved in organizing the workshop “Tailored to Move: Wearable Robotics for Motion Assistance”, which will take place at [IEEE ICRA 2026](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/) in Vienna, Austria.
---
The full-day workshop will bring together researchers and practitioners working on different aspects of wearable robotics, including hardware design, modeling and control strategies, learning and simulation, applications, and regulatory considerations. The event aims to provide an interactive forum for discussion and exchange across disciplines within the wearable robotics community.

The program will feature invited talks by leading experts, live demonstrations of wearable robotic systems, poster sessions with flash presentations, and panel discussions on current challenges and opportunities in the field. Additional activities will include student mentoring sessions and networking opportunities, with hybrid participation available to enable broader accessibility.

The workshop is supported by the IEEE Technical Committee on Wearable Robotics. Find out more in the workshop's [website](https://sites.google.com/view/tailored-to-move-icra2026/home).
