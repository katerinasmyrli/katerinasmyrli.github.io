> Our full-day workshop [Tailored to Move](https://sites.google.com/view/tailored-to-move-icra2026/home) took place today in the context of [IEEE ICRA 2026](https://2026.ieee-icra.org/) in Vienna, Austria.

---
As one of the workshop's organizers, I was very happy to see the room full throughout the full-day event.
The workshop included multiple presentations from leading experts in wearable robotics, a thought-provoking panel on the future of wearables, as well as poster and demo competitions!
I enjoyed catching up with colleagues and making a lot of new acquaintances.

Thanks to everyone for their participation in making this such a memorable event!
