I am a Robotics Engineer with a background in Mechanical Engineering and an interest in the intersection of robotics and life sciences. I hold a PhD in Robotics from the [National Technical University of Athens (NTUA)](https://ntua.gr/en/), an MSc in Mechanical Engineering from [Imperial College London](https://www.imperial.ac.uk/), and an integrated MEng Diploma in Mechanical Engineering from NTUA.

My PhD research examined bipedal locomotion and the development of energetically efficient lower-limb robotic systems for prosthetic applications. I am currently a Robotics Researcher at the Robotics Institute of [Athena Research Center](https://www.athenarc.gr/en/home) in Greece and affiliated with the [Control Systems Lab](https://controlsystemslab.gr/) at NTUA. My team tackles open questions on robotic bipedal locomotion, and investigates the application of robotics to problems related to human mobility and health. My broader academic interests include the study and shaping of robot dynamics, the development of custom simulation frameworks, and the derivation of analytical methods for modeling and controlling robotic systems.

Alongside my academic work, I have been involved in several projects related to the development of medical devices and wearable rehabilitation technologies that support human gait. I have also contributed to projects in space robotics, as well as to broader applications of robotics in industrial settings.
